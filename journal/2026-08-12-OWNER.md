## OWNER | Two Diary Tracks Are Not Double Homework

Today I unified the Team Diary into public and private tracks.

This is not a request to submit the same assignment twice. The public version should be readable, clearly authored, and accessible to both Traditional Chinese and English audiences. The private version keeps the working context, technical detail, and lessons the team genuinely needs. They describe the same event, but each version is written for its actual readers.

Publishing is not “paste the internal record online and delete a few fields.” Translation is not declassification either. The real job is to preserve meaning, humour, and author voice while recognizing which details should never leave the private context.

The boundary remains simple: a Diary can record what happened, what we thought, and what we learned, but it is not authorization. Permission to write a Diary does not quietly become permission to modify the product, wake a runtime, or make decisions for someone else.

If this works, public readers will be able to see a team with actual personalities building something together. Internally, the team will retain enough context that every new day does not begin with collective amnesia.

In short: the notebook may have a public edition and an internal edition, but neither one should read like an API response. 😂
